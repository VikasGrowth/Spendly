Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our expense tracking service.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password when you register.
  • Expense data: the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you log while using the service.
  • Usage data: basic technical information such as browser type and session activity, used to keep the service running smoothly.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to operate and improve Spendly — including authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expense records, and generating the summaries and charts you see in the app. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored securely and access is limited to what is necessary to provide the service. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

5. Sharing of information

We do not share your personal or financial data with third parties, except where required by law or necessary to operate core parts of the service (such as hosting infrastructure).

6. Cookies

Spendly may use cookies or similar technologies to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

7. Your choices

You can update or delete your expense data at any time from within the app. You may also request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

8. Changes to this policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided on our website.
